El Mogy Fresh — Organic E-Commerce Platform

ASP.NET Core 10 MVC e-commerce platform built with 4-Layer Clean Architecture & Domain-Driven Design (DDD).

1. Business Context & Problem Statement

Fresh produce retailers require a reliable online shopping experience where guest visitors can add products to their shopping cart without registering upfront. However, traditional e-commerce implementations often discard guest carts upon login or fail to clean up abandoned session records in the database.

2. Core Requirements & Scope

  • Hybrid Shopping Cart: Session/cookie guest carts auto-merge into registered user profiles upon login.
  • Multi-Language Support: Arabic and English interface switching via `LanguageController`.
  • Automated Background Cleanup: Background services for clearing expired guest carts without locking active tables.
  • Catalog & Orders: Category filters, coupons, order status tracking, and PDF invoices.

3. Chosen Architecture & Trade-offs

Structured into 4 decoupled projects: `FreshVeg.Domain`, `FreshVeg.Application`, `FreshVeg.Infrastructure`, and `FreshVeg.Web`.

Architectural Trade-off

Using 4 separate project layers increased initial setup time compared to a monolithic MVC project, but guaranteed strict boundary isolation between business rules and EF Core persistence logic.
